line is, and use it as a signal of movement ("near a strong support, buy in").

## The journey (what we tried, what happened)
1. **v1** `detector.py` — swing-pivot + volume-strength; proximity as a bracket entry.
   NULL: distance→forward-R flat/non-monotonic, "strength" ANTI-selects. Brand: too
   naive — ignored the dips/rejection between touches.
2. **v2** `detector_v2.py` — rejection-confirmed zones (anchor→depart≥D ATR→retest).
   respect-rate-vs-random sweep (`eval_sweep.py`/`gen_grid.py`, 68 combos) → **~0 lift**
   (`results_all.csv`). Brand's critique = the breakthrough: anchored on single-bar
   SPIKES not consolidation SHELVES, counted touches one-sided, locked the line to the
   pivot extreme not the price price keeps reacting at, AND tested on a trend-heavy
   universe (washes any S/R edge to zero). The v1/v2 nulls are about the OLD detector.
3. **v3** `detector_v3.py` (CURRENT detector) — shelf anchors (flat closes → ≥breakaway
   ATR departure), polarity-agnostic touches (S+R one pool, flip ON), magnet center
   (reaction-magnitude-weighted → migrates to the price actually reacted at;
   independently reproduced Brand's hand-picked AAPL ~247 level), recency-weighted
   strength (~3mo halflife), non-max-suppression dedupe (one line per ~1 ATR).
   Two real bugs fixed: (a) a lookahead leak via the evolving vol-weighted center;
   (b) touches were dated at the DEPARTURE bar (up to ~12 bars after the shelf) so they
   "looked un-consolidated" — now dated at the shelf bar + actual shelf price. PIT
   verified bit-identical full-vs-truncated. Brand eyeball-validated (~17:7 reversal
   hits on AAPL).
4. **profile** `profile.py` — Brand's "banding" idea = Volume Profile: recency+volume
   weighted price histogram, scipy `find_peaks`. Peaks = levels. Better LOCATOR
   (objective, parameter-light); both methods agree on dominant levels (PFE ~24.5, KO ~69).
5. **fusion** `fusion.py` — level LOCATION = profile peak, kept only if ≥2 shelf
   REACTIONS sit within `snap_atr` (drops pass-through nodes, Brand's VZ 37.44). Brand
   greenlit this as the approach.
6. **recovery** `recovery.py` — after a touch, +2 ATR win = MEDIAN 7 trading days
   (IQR 4–12), +1.5 ATR ~5d, full bounce (median MFE 3.7 ATR) peaks ~20 bars/4wk →
   "2–4 week swing trades." CAVEAT: this study ignored stops (too rosy).
7. **bounce_sim** `bounce_sim.py` (LATEST, the honest test) — long-only, STOP-aware,
   enters on every pullback retest into a confirmed support (counts the breaks, not
   just bounces), vs a random-nearby-line baseline. 60 range-bound names, 2,186 trades:

   | stop/tp (ATR) | trades/sym-yr | win% | stop% | mean R | random baseline |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1.0 / 2.0 | 4.1 | 36% | **60%** | −0.030 | +0.108 ❌ |
   | 1.5 / 2.0 | 4.1 | 44% | **47%** | +0.021 | +0.062 ❌ |
   | 1.0 / 3.0 | 4.1 | 24% | **66%** | −0.046 | +0.046 ❌ |

   **The naive bounce is OUT:** stopped 47–66% of the time (Brand's exact worry), and
   the real S/R level LOSES to a random nearby line — anchoring to the actual level adds
   nothing. Faint general edge in "buy a pullback in a range-bound name" (~+0.05–0.11R)
   is beta, not S/R. Frequency is fine (~4/sym-yr × 60 ≈ 240/yr) when you count retests
   not formations (Brand's "10 trades" was the formation count).

## WHERE WE STOPPED — open doors (Brand's own observations point here; NOT tested)
1. **Wait for the flatten, don't catch the knife (recommended next).** bounce_sim
   entered on FIRST contact with the zone = knife-catching = why it stopped out. Brand's
   favorite setups were "falling knife *flattens out* THEN spikes" — a confirmation.
   Test: enter only AFTER a shelf forms *at* the level (consolidation confirmed), not on
   first touch. Directly attacks the 47–66% stop-out. Modify `bounce_sim.py`'s entry
   trigger from "low enters zone" to "a flat shelf (à la detect_events) forms in the zone."
2. **Follow the break, don't fade it.** 60% break rate + Brand's 5-long/2-short breakout
   count → the edge may be in trading the BREAK (momentum) not the bounce (reversion).
   Long-only: trade upside breaks of resistance-turned-support.
3. (Lower priority) Detection density: PFE fused to only 2 levels (it's single-noded);
   Brand felt fusion over-filtered and missed an obvious 2018–19 support (recency
   weighting fades old levels). Loosen `prominence_frac`/`recency_halflife` if pursuing
   more levels, but the edge question (doors 1–2) matters more than more lines.
